# Monetization

## Business Model

AITimeline should charge for hosted convenience and AI usage, not for the existence of the core idea.

## Free

- Limited daily knowledge cards.
- Limited AI comments.
- Manual source selection.
- Local or lightweight graph.

Purpose: let users feel the product loop before asking for money.

## Pro

Suggested first price range: RMB 29 to 59 per month.

Includes:

- higher AI interaction quota
- automatic agent refresh
- more saved cards
- multi-device sync
- review queue
- richer graph views

## Deep Research Add-On

Use credits for expensive jobs:

- long web research
- paper/report synthesis
- topic maps
- source comparison
- weekly knowledge briefing

## BYO Key Plan

For technical users:

- user provides their own model provider key
- app charges a smaller fee for UI, sync, graph and review
- useful for community trust and cost control

## Cost Rules

- Treat AI interaction as metered from day one.
- Cache summaries and embeddings aggressively.
- Use cheaper models for extraction and routing.
- Reserve stronger models for deep research and difficult explanations.
- Put search-heavy workflows behind quota or credits.

## Store Risk

If distributing via App Store or Google Play, subscription pricing must include store service fees. Also avoid copycat UI and metadata that imply connection to X or any other third-party service.
